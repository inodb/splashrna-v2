SplashRNA

Pick potent miR-E shRNAs for a target. Paste a sequence, or look up a human gene by ID or RefSeq accession, and you get back the best-scoring guides with their 97-mer constructs ready to order.

Scoring happens in your browser, so sequences you paste stay on your machine. The only thing ever sent out is a RefSeq accession, to fetch that transcript from NCBI.

What do you want to target?

One target per FASTA record. Each can be a nucleotide sequence, a human gene ID (7157), or a RefSeq accession (NM_…).

Academic use

If you use these predictions, please cite Pelossof R, Fairchild L, et al. Prediction of potent shRNAs with a sequential classification algorithm. Nature Biotechnology 35, 350–353 (2017), and add to your methods: “The shRNAs were designed using the SplashRNA algorithm (Pelossof and Fairchild et al., 2017).”

These are the published 2017 classifiers, rebuilt to run without their original dependencies. Scores match the ones the original server produced to within 10−6.
