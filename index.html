SplashRNA

Prediction of potent shRNAs with a sequential classification algorithm. Everything runs in your browser — no sequence you enter leaves this page, except when you ask for a transcript by accession.

Input

One or more FASTA records. Each record may hold a nucleotide sequence, a RefSeq accession (NM_…), or an Entrez gene ID.

Options

Method: Pelossof R, Fairchild L, et al. Prediction of potent shRNAs with a sequential classification algorithm. Nature Biotechnology 35, 350–353 (2017).

A rewrite of the original SplashRNA server. The classifiers are the published 2017 models, re-expressed without their original dependencies; scores are reproduced to within 10−6 of the originals.
